    How to play

    • Type to run, space to jump

      Every correct letter carries Claude forward, and words move on by themselves. Space is only for jumping. Press it again in the air for a double or triple jump. On a phone, tap the sky to jump.

    • Creatures come at you

      They walk toward you whether you type or not. Jump over them, or land on the small ones to stomp them. Each one has its own trick.

      Beetle
      jump or stomp · ½ heart
      Moth
      flies at head height, don't jump · ½ heart
      Hopper
      bounces, time it · ½ heart
      Code Stack
      double jump · 1 heart
      Glitch Giant
      triple jump · 1½ hearts

    • Outrun the wave

      The wave keeps pace with your typing and speeds up as you get faster. If it catches you, you lose a heart. Keep typing while you jump so it can't catch up.

    • Sparkles, hearts and combos

      Golden words push the wave back. Heart words restore a heart. Type 25 letters in a row without a mistake to raise your multiplier, up to x8.

    • Waves and worlds

      Every 20 seconds brings a new wave, with harder words and new creatures. Every two waves the scenery changes too. For pure speed, try a 15, 30 or 60 second Sprint, where nothing comes at you.
